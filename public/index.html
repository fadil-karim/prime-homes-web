About us

A family-run property group, rooted in south west London.

Established in 2000, Prime Group is a family-run real estate development and investment business based primarily in south west London. We specialize in building and delivering high quality build to rent homes and semi commercial properties.

Our team identifies underutilized sites and we take a collaborative approach with local councils and communities to create welcoming and sustainable places to live and work. Recently the group is also exploring real estate opportunities abroad with a view of market diversification.

  • Our in-house estate agency and property management team provide aftercare service to the group's property portfolio.
  • Managing our own homes keeps a pulse on local market trends and gives valuable insight into arising opportunities and challenges.
  • Registered with the ICO and affiliated with the DPS, NRLA, Property Redress Scheme, RLA and UKALA.
