Property description

This well presented apartment has been finished to a good standard with good proportions and plenty of natural light. This property boasts in excess of 800 sq ft of a charming double fronted building. The property comprises three bedrooms one with an en-suite, separate modern bathroom, open plan living/kitchen and bi fold doors that lead you onto a private garden.

Dafforne Road is a quiet residential road in the heart of Tooting Bec. Local amenities, bars and restaurants are in abundance as well as being perfectly located for some outstanding schools. Both Tooting Bec Station (Northern line, zone 3) and local bus routes are within easy reach.
